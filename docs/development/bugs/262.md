## Description

`toPyAddr` and `toPyAddrWithPort` share identical `AF_INET`/`AF_INET6`/`AF_UNIX` branches; only `self.getPort()` vs passed `port: u16` differs. Pseudosocket `getsockname`/`getpeername` duplicate `AF_UNIX` path-length check and `Address.toPyAddr` fallback. `set_future_exception` is copy-pasted 3 times.

## Trigger

Maintenance: fixing `BUG-171` (port bounds) in `fromPyAddr` required patching `toPyAddr` family too, but only one copy was updated initially in audit history.

## Consequences

- Fixes applied to one copy silently missing in the other → regression.
- Binary bloat; two `inet_ntop` call sites to audit for `PyErr_Occurred`.

## Fix (Not Applied)

Factor `toPyAddrWithPort` to call `toPyAddr` with `self.setPort(port)` on a copy, or extract helper `formatAddress(family, hostBuf, port)`.
